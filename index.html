What We Do

The Taping & Finishing Specialists

After the board goes up, that's where Steve comes in. Every seam taped, every coat smoothed, every corner crisp.

Taping & Mudding

This is what STS was built on. Bed tape, fill coats, and finish coats over every flat joint, butt joint, inside corner, outside corner, and fastener head. Multiple passes with the right compound for zero visibility.

Flat Joints Butt Joints Inside Corners Outside Corners Fasteners Metal/Paper Corner Bead

Finish Levels 1–5

Level 1 for garages and utility spaces. Level 4 for most painted walls. Level 5 for high-end paint, gloss finishes, and areas with strong side-lighting. We match the grade to the job.

Texture Application

Knockdown, orange peel, skip trowel, slap brush, smooth — we match existing textures perfectly or apply new ones to spec.

Drywall Repair

Holes, cracks, water damage, nail pops, failed previous tape jobs — we cut it out, retape it, and blend it so you'd never know it was touched.

Ceiling Finishing

Flat ceilings, vaulted, tray, coffered — all the overhead seams and corners that most tapers hate. We tape them tight and finish them right.
